FAQS

Event details & frequently asked questions

1. What is Hackathon 3.0?

Hackathon 3.0 is a 8-hour hackathon where participants transform their ideas into working solutions through ideation, development, and deployment.

2. Who can participate in the hackathon?

Hackathon 3.0 is open to all students who are interested in innovation, technology, and problem-solving.

3. When and where will Hackathon 3.0 take place?

Hackathon 3.0 will be held on October 11, 2026, at DYPIT, Pimpri.

  • Time: 9:00 AM – 7:00 PM
  • Venue: 5th Floor Labs, DYPIT, Pimpri (subject to change)

4. What should I bring to the hackathon?

Participants are required to carry their participation ticket.

Laptops are optional, as PCs will be provided at the venue. Participants who prefer to use their own devices may do so.

5. What is the registration fee?

The registration fee depends on the team size:

  • Solo: ₹79
  • Duo: ₹139
  • Team of 3–5: ₹249

(PS:An additional 5% platform fee will be applicable to the registration fee.)
