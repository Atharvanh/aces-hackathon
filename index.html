Problem Statements

FAQS

Event details & frequently asked questions

Who can take part?

Students from any branch can join. Final eligibility details will be announced by the club.

Do I need hackathon experience?

Beginners are welcome. Bring your curiosity and a willingness to learn with your team.

How many people can be on a team?

Team size limits will be shared when registration opens.

What should I bring?

Bring your laptop, charger, student ID, and anything you need to work comfortably.

Can we use existing code or libraries?

Open-source tools and libraries may be used with proper credit. The final event rules will explain what is allowed.

How will projects be judged?

Projects may be assessed on creativity, usefulness, implementation, and presentation. The final criteria will be published before the event.

Where do I register?

Use Register Now on the Home screen once the registration link is available.

Who can I contact for help?

Club contact details and support channels will be added here before the event.

Console controls

Use the D-pad or arrow keys to choose a menu item. Press Enter to select. Home or Escape returns to Home. The square button (or M key) mutes/unmutes audio. The right-hand buttons are reserved for socials.
