    About HACKATHON 3.0 · Organized by ACES

    About HACKATHON 3.0

    Welcome to HACKATHON 3.0, the flagship hackathon hosted by the Association of Computer Engineering Students (ACES) at Dr. D. Y. Patil Institute of Technology, Pimpri.

    HACKATHON 3.0 is designed to move beyond traditional API wrappers and prompt engineering. This year, the focus shifts to local execution, architectural control, and real-time model serving. Participants are challenged to take open-source models from Hugging Face, run them locally on raw hardware, and architect robust, scalable API layers to power real-world applications.

    Whether you are an infrastructure enthusiast optimizing inference latency, a full-stack developer wiring up dynamic user interfaces, or an AI builder experimenting with open weights, HACKATHON 3.0 is built to test your engineering depth under realistic sprint conditions.

    The Challenge: Local AI Meets API Architecture

    Cloud APIs have made AI integration accessible, but real-world engineering often demands privacy, zero external dependency, and low latency.

    At HACKATHON 3.0, you won't rely on third-party cloud credits or external endpoints. You will:

    • Harness Open Weights: Deploy locally hosted foundational models directly from the Hugging Face hub.
    • Build Clean Integrations: Wrap models inside performant backend APIs (REST, gRPC, or WebSockets) for seamless frontend or system-level consumption.
    • Tackle Applied Scenarios: Build functional, end-to-end prototypes around 5 distinct real-world problem statements (to be revealed on hack day).
